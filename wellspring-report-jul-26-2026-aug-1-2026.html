Key Observations
Best cost per enquiry this year at $48 — lower spend, stronger results. This week's budget reduction was planned, but the outcome was better than expected: 6 Google leads at $48 each, vs 5 leads at $106 each last week. DSA held at $2.17 per click and Broad averaged $4.44. As budgets scale back up in August, the priority should be rebuilding Broad Match first since DSA is already working efficiently within its budget.
Medicaid has now appeared for the 6th consecutive week — this cannot wait any longer. Each week, at least one person contacts Wellspring through Google Ads with Medicaid insurance and cannot be seen. Adding negative keywords (medicaid, priority medicaid, state insurance, government insurance) across both campaigns will stop these enquiries before they reach the intake team. This is the single most overdue action item on the account.
Two referral leads couldn't be seen because Wellspring doesn't treat substance abuse. Both came via external referrals — not through ads, so this can't be filtered at the campaign level. However, it may be worth communicating service scope to any GP or PCP practices that are actively referring patients, so they can pre-screen for substance abuse before sending contacts through. Saves the intake team time on both sides.
What’s Next
🚨 Immediate — Add Medicaid negative keywords (6 weeks overdue)
Add to both DSA and Broad Match: “medicaid”, “priority medicaid”, “state insurance”, “government insurance”. This is a 5-minute account change that should have been made weeks ago. No further delay.
📈 August — Scale Broad Match budget back up
Budget was reduced in late July to manage the monthly overage. From August, plan to restore Broad to $60–$70/day to capture more of the qualified traffic Google is finding. This week's $48 CPL shows the campaigns are in a strong position to scale.
