Key Observations
External and Psychology Today referrals booked at 100% — 3 of 3 converted. Both word-of-mouth leads and the Psychology Today contact all scheduled successfully. Referral quality continues to outperform paid channels on conversion rate. The Psychology Today lead booked for Medication Management — a higher-value service type.

These are filterable — adding Medicare and addiction-related terms as negatives would reduce this type of spend going forward. Neither reflects an issue with ad targeting or lead quality.
