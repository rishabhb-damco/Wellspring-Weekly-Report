Key Observations
No appointments were booked from Google Ads this week — the first time on record. All 4 Google leads had a specific disqualifying issue: one was on Medicaid (still not filtered by negative keywords after 7 weeks), two stopped mid-inquiry at the insurance step, and one cited cost as a barrier. The click and impression numbers were healthy (113 clicks, 6.34% CTR) so the campaigns are delivering traffic — but the leads are not converting. Each failure reason has a specific fix.
Medicaid has appeared for the 7th consecutive week — this is now a critical account issue. The UHC Medicaid block (Lead 1, Aug 3) is the same problem that has appeared every single week since mid-June. Adding “medicaid”, “UHC medicaid”, “state insurance”, and “government insurance” as negative keywords takes under 5 minutes in the Google Ads interface. If this is not done before next Monday, it will appear for an 8th week.
3 leads dropped off at the insurance verification step mid-inquiry. Leads 4, 12, and 13 all started the inquiry process but stopped when they reached the insurance section. These came via Google Ads, Internal, and Psychology Today respectively — so it's not a source-specific issue. It may be worth reviewing the inquiry form with Wellspring to see if insurance eligibility can be surfaced earlier (e.g., a simple dropdown before the full form) to reduce friction and save time for both patients and intake staff.
