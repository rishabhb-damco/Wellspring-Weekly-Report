DSA was 58% cheaper per click than Generic/Broad Match on average. Despite this, DSA's daily budget cap ($15/day) limited it to at most 20% of total spend every week. At DSA's CPC efficiency, doubling its budget allocation would have generated an estimated 4–6 additional leads per week at lower cost.
Notable campaign events this quarter
Jun 21–27: DSA conflict resolved. DSA and Generic had been alternating delivery (not running the same day) for weeks prior. Fixed in this week — both ran all 7 days for the first time.
Jul 5–11: DSA absent entire week. Zero spend across 7 days — cause investigated (change history). Led to ~3–5 missed enquiries. Restored Jul 12–18.
Aug 9–15: Third campaign launched (New Broad Match). 3-campaign structure ran for 1 week alongside DSA + Generic. Broad Match paused end of that week; account returned to 2-campaign structure.
Aug 19: Medicaid negative keywords added. 8 consecutive weeks with a Medicaid-blocked lead resolved in one 5-minute account change. Jul 19–25 was the only prior exception (no Medicaid block that week).

Three Themes That Defined the Quarter
1. The Medicaid problem cost the quarter dearly
Medicaid-blocked leads appeared in 8 of 12 weeks — roughly 1 unqualified lead per week, costing an estimated $480–$640 in wasted spend over the quarter. The fix (negative keywords) took 8 weeks to implement. Adding it on Day 1 would have saved money and improved CPL throughout.
2. DSA was consistently the most efficient campaign — and chronically under-budgeted
DSA's CPC averaged $2.47 vs $5.96 for Generic/Broad — 58% cheaper per click, every single week. Its $15/day cap meant it never exceeded 20% of total spend. Doubling DSA's daily budget was the highest-ROI opportunity that sat on the table all quarter.
3. Scheduling rate is partly a capacity issue, not just a campaign issue
Internal referrals booked at 80%+ every week. Google Ads averaged 54%. The gap isn't quality — it's that Google leads sometimes face capacity constraints (ADHD testing waitlist, male therapist availability, specialist gaps) that internal referrals navigate around. Some of the "low" weeks were Wellspring-side, not campaign-side.
