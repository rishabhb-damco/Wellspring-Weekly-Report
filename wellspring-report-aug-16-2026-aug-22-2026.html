Key Observations
Best scheduling rate in weeks — 60% overall, and 100% from Google Ads. Nine of 15 leads booked this week, up from 7 of 16 last week. Internal referrals had an exceptional week with 5 leads all converting. Google Ads contacts both booked. The improved rate reflects better lead quality across sources.
CPL has risen to $237 after three weeks below $150. With only 2 Google Ads enquiries this week against $474 in spend, the cost per lead is the highest it’s been in the tracked period. Both leads converted, so quality held — but volume needs to recover. Watch next week to see if this is a one-off dip or the start of a trend.
DSA drove 7 phone call extension clicks but 0 form fill enquiries this week. At $2.90/click and $113 total spend, DSA appears to be attracting phone intent rather than form intent. Phone callers historically convert at a higher rate (~21% vs lower for forms). Worth confirming with Wellspring whether any of these 7 calls resulted in a booked appointment.
