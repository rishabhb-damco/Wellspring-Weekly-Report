What We’re Focusing on in October
Maximising October demand
Historical lead data shows seasonal demand typically picks up in October. We will be positioning the campaigns to capitalise on this — ensuring budgets are ready, bids are competitive, and ad copy is aligned with the inbound intent spike as it arrives.
Landing page conversion update
We are actively working on updating the Wellspring landing page to improve the conversion rate from click to enquiry. The goal is to ensure that the stronger October traffic translates into more booked appointments, not just more clicks.
Cultural landing page for Asian patients
We have a dedicated landing page built for Asian-background patients, designed around culturally relevant messaging. This will be activated and tested once generic keyword search demand returns to a level where it can receive meaningful traffic.
