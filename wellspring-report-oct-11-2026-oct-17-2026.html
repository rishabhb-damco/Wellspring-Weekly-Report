Recommended Actions

Priority items heading into week of 19 Oct 2026
High priority
Investigate “Missing Keywords” flag on Generic Search
Google flagged Generic Search as "Eligible (Limited) — missing enough relevant keywords" all 7 days this week. Review the Search Terms report to understand if this is a genuine gap or an automated recommendation to add broad match keywords. Do not add new keywords without reviewing match types — this could dilute targeting.
Medium priority
DSA budget at $10/day — reassess against CPC efficiency
DSA generated 6 phone extension clicks + 2 platform conversions on just $70 spend. CPC was $1.32 vs Generic's $5.93. If the budget was recently cut from $15 to $10, the rationale needs review — DSA is the most efficient click source in the account.
Medium priority
Add negative keywords for controlled substances
L4 was seeking klonopin (a Schedule IV controlled substance). Wellspring does not prescribe controlled substances. Add keywords like "klonopin", "xanax", "adderall", "benzodiazepine", "prescription refill" as negative exact matches to both campaigns to filter this intent upstream.
Watch
Monitor CPL trend — 4th consecutive week above $89
CPL has risen: $69 → $106 → $89 → $135 over the past 4 weeks. This is partly a small-sample effect (4 Google leads this week), but the pattern warrants attention. If spend remains at $500+ and Google leads stay below 5/week, October CPL will significantly exceed the August benchmark of ~$99.

Both bookings (L2 Nicholas, L3 Noah) came via organic phone call with blank source — consistent with the DSA phone-call engagement pattern, though not traceable directly to a specific campaign ad.

This is a strong signal that DSA's call extension is actively driving phone intent — but the $10/day budget cap means this channel ran out of room well before its potential. If any of these 6 calls converted to appointments, the true DSA CPL could be very low.
Low lead volume week (5 total) — context needed. Oct 11–17 is the lowest lead volume in recent weeks. The two non-bookings were both service scope mismatches — quality of actual enquiries was strong. If volume stays below 6 next week, a budget or targeting review is warranted.
