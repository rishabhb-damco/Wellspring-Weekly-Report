Analysis
Three Signals from August
📉
Efficiency Up, Quality Down
CPL dropped 44% ($172 → $96) and lead volume grew 50%. But booking rate fell 16 percentage points. More leads at lower cost reflects the winding-down of the Broad Match test — but the quality filter hasn't caught up. Negative keyword coverage and ad copy qualification need to close the gap before October budget decisions are made.
🚫
Insurance Exclusions: Still the #1 Blocker
55% of Google Ads non-bookings in August were insurance mismatches — Medicaid ×3, Cigna ×2, Medicare ×1. The Medicaid negatives added Aug 19 worked: zero Medicaid blocks after that date. But Cigna, Medicare, and HAP remain uncovered. HAP has blocked leads in multiple weeks and has been an open action for 4+ months with no resolution.
📞
DSA Call Attribution Gap
DSA recorded 7+ call extension clicks in the Aug 30–Sep 5 week with 0 Google Ads form conversions — the third consecutive week of this pattern. Either call-driven bookings aren't being attributed in the platform (tracking gap), or the calls aren't converting. This needs a conversion tracking audit before DSA's CPL figures are used for budget reallocation decisions.
Actions
September Priorities
Critical · This Week
01
Add Medicare + Addiction Negatives
Add "medicare", "medicare insurance", "addiction treatment", "substance abuse", "drug rehab" as negatives across both active campaigns. Both triggered unqualified August leads that Medicaid negatives didn't catch. Fast, low-risk fix with immediate impact on lead quality.
Critical · ASAP — Open 4+ Months
02
Resolve HAP Insurance Status
HAP has blocked multiple leads across multiple weeks. Open since April 2026 with no resolution. A confirmed yes/no from Wellspring unlocks a patient segment or enables a HAP-specific negative keyword. Every week without an answer is a week of wasted ad spend on a filterable decline reason.
Critical · This Month
03
Fix Paperwork Completion Drop-Off
35% of leads who schedule a consultation never complete intake paperwork — 283 scheduled vs 184 completed (Dec 2025–Aug 2026). No campaign optimisation recovers revenue lost here. A reminder sequence or simplified paperwork process on Wellspring's side is the fix; this is not an ad problem.
High · This Month
04
Audit the Booking Rate Decline
Overall booking rate fell from 63% to 47% month-over-month. Insurance mismatches explain part of the drop, but non-Google sources also booked at only 46% in August — suggesting a clinic-side capacity factor. Confirm with Wellspring whether August had fewer available appointment slots before attributing the full decline to ad quality.
