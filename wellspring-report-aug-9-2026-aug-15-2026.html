Key Observations
A new campaign is running and driving the majority of spend — but it's untracked. Damco_WS_Generic_Search_090626_Broad ran all 7 days and accounted for $355 (68%) of the week's budget, delivering 5 form conversions and 4 phone call clicks. The old Generic Search (041125) also ran Aug 12–15, creating a 4-day overlap. This should be clarified with the Google Ads manager — if the new campaign is the intended primary, the old one should be paused to avoid split budgets.
Medicaid appeared for the 8th consecutive week — now 3 blocked leads in a single week. Lead 1 (Medicaid), Lead 4 (Blue Cross Complete — a Medicaid-managed plan), and Lead 14 (Meridian Medicaid) all failed due to insurance. This is the first week with 3 Medicaid blocks in one period. Adding negatives — “medicaid”, “meridian health”, “state insurance”, “government insurance” — to both active campaigns takes under 5 minutes. Every week without this action is a confirmed, avoidable cost.
Two no-call no-shows in one week — a pattern worth addressing with Wellspring. Lead 5 (Aug 5 inquiry, Aug 12 consultation) and Lead 11 (Aug 13 inquiry, Aug 14 consultation) both had bookings that went unattended. Follow-up calls were made on Aug 14 and 17 for Lead 5, and Aug 17 for Lead 11. A 24-hour confirmation text or call before each consultation could reduce NCNS significantly — this is a Wellspring intake process improvement, not a campaign issue.
