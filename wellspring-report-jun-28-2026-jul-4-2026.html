Key Observations
Independence Day week — low volume is expected, not a concern. July 4th consistently suppresses therapy search intent across the US. The 6 leads and $141 CPL this week are not representative of the true campaign performance level. The Jun 14–21 week ($49 CPL, 12 leads) remains the more reliable baseline to judge against.
Medicaid is now blocking leads for the 4th week in a row — this requires an immediate fix. Two more Medicaid-insured people contacted Wellspring this week and could not be accepted. Adding negative keywords for “Medicaid” and related terms will filter these out at the ad level, reducing wasted spend and saving intake team time on calls that cannot convert.
Broad Match CPC spiked to $19.92 on July 3 — investigate the search terms report. The campaign spent $99.59 on just 5 clicks that day, nearly 3× the weekly average. Smart Bidding may have over-bid on a highly competitive query. Reviewing the Jul 3 search terms and setting a target CPA or max CPC cap on the Broad campaign would prevent this from recurring.
What’s Next
🚨 Immediate — Add Medicaid negative keywords
Add to both DSA and Broad Match campaigns: “medicaid”, “priority medicaid”, “state insurance”, “government insurance”, “medicaid therapist”. This single action will prevent a recurring source of unqualified leads and improve the effective cost per qualified enquiry.
🔍 Exploring additional platforms to scale leads
With Google Ads performing well in normal weeks (Jun 14–21: 12 leads, $49 CPL), the next step is evaluating Meta (Facebook & Instagram), Psychology Today premium listings, and YouTube to open additional patient acquisition channels and reduce dependence on search-only traffic.
